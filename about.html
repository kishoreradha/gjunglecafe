About G' Jungle Cafe

Welcome to G' Jungle Cafe, a unique dining and event destination located in the heart of La Carlota City, Negros Occidental.

We are passionate about bringing people together through great food, memorable celebrations, and exceptional hospitality.

Whether you are visiting for our Unlimited Samgyeopsal experience, a family gathering, a birthday celebration,

or a corporate event, our goal is to make every occasion special and unforgettable.

Our spacious venue provides a relaxing atmosphere where friends, families, and colleagues can enjoy quality time together while creating lasting memories.
